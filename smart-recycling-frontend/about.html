About SRMMS

Innovating waste management through smart technology, community rewards, and sustainable eco-action.

🎯

Our Mission

To revolutionize recycling accessibility through smart automated reverse-vending tech, encouraging sustainable daily habits by providing instant monetary and reward incentives.

🌱

Our Vision

To build a zero-waste future across Sri Lanka and beyond, empowering educational institutions, local municipalities, and citizens to achieve environmental sustainability together.

💡

Our Innovation

Combining IoT hardware sensors with a seamless software platform that automatically measures weight, segregates waste, and credits digital reward points to user profiles.
