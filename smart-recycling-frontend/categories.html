What Can You Recycle?

Turn waste into value. Bring your recyclable items and help create a cleaner, greener planet.

🌎 🍃 🌿
🍾

Plastic

Plastic waste can be recycled and turned into new products, reducing pollution and conserving resources.

Accepted Examples

  • PET bottles (water, soda)
  • Plastic containers
  • Packaging plastic, bags
Rate per Kg Rs. 15.00
♻

Paper

Paper recycling saves trees, reduces landfill waste and conserves energy and water.

Accepted Examples

  • Newspapers & magazines
  • Cardboard boxes
  • Office paper, cartons
Rate per Kg Rs. 12.00
🫙

Glass

Glass can be recycled endlessly without losing quality, helping to save natural resources.

Accepted Examples

  • Glass bottles
  • Jars
  • Broken glass (clean)
Rate per Kg Rs. 30.00
🥫

Metal

Metal recycling saves energy, reduces mining and helps preserve natural resources.

Accepted Examples

  • Aluminium cans
  • Steel cans
  • Metal containers
Rate per Kg Rs. 35.00
